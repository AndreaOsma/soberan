# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation to be harassment-free and professional for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive in feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologising when appropriate.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others’ private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions (comments, issues, pull requests) that breach this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in being blocked from the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private reporting paths where applicable).
